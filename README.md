# Summeet ⛰️

Summeet is a dating&meeting app, but instead of the bar you hit the mountain.

A couple of angles make summeet unique:
- the stress on genuinity rather than performance. On purpose the search is about hikes -the content and the main activity on a summeet date- rather than profile pics
- the idea to rely on a community: from the start it is built-in the app an ongoing feed for any summeet users: all can write, anyone can read.
- alternative dating: both because not many dating apps have mountains as a background, but also because a hike -or date- should not officially start unless at least 3 people are there. This pushes users to redefine the idea of a date and gives a new spin to the more the merrier idea.

And most importantly, Summeet is built to please, since it starts as a gift.
Enjoy it!
